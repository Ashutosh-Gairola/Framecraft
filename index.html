How it works

From a paragraph to a film

  1. 01

    Write the scene

    Paste a script, a timeline, or one line. The studio turns it into a shot sheet: holds, the expensive shot, a closed colour script, the cast, the voice.

  2. 02

    The crew draws it

    Your agent (Claude, Codex, Cursor, or Grok) runs the crew: research references first, design, act, light, and shoot, in 2D cels or full three.js worlds.

  3. 03

    Score, review, render

    Sound is synthesized from the action itself. An editor checks stutter, cuts, colour and loudness, then HyperFrames renders at 24 fps.

The crew

Every role is a skill

Each role is a skill file your agent reads, under skills/ in the repo.

New · the character system

One body, sculpted and rigged from code

People aren't stacks of tubes any more. Every character is one continuous skin, sculpted from anatomical masses, with a face (brow, nose, lips, eyelids), hair and clothes grown from the same surface, and a real skeleton down to the fingers.

  • Design from a reference photo, or from a known character's signature look
  • The same design renders realistic or anime (cel shading with ink outlines)
  • A turnaround gate: nothing animates until the model sheet passes
