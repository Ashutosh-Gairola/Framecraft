## Light and grade

Moonless night, calm nearshore water, then open dark water. Preserve a near-black foreground and saturated navy midground; reserve cyan for small, localized plankton flashes. Smithsonian's material describes dinoflagellate light as stimulus/clock-related, not permanent outlines. No reference still was sampled; colors map to the closed sheet and its three design colors, including a brighter navy wash for the open-water midground.

## Movement references

Sea turtle swim: slow 48-frame cycle, long downstroke for propulsion and eased recovery; smaller hind flippers steer. Fish school: smooth shared turn and subtle tail oscillation, no ground contact. Plankton and bubbles drift on independent seeded paths; brief flashes follow nearby water disturbance.
