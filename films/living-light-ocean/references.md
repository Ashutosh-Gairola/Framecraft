## Light and grade

Night, faint moon low on the horizon, stars. Above water: navy sky with haze at the horizon, black wet sand, glow only on the breaking lines. Underwater: no sunlight; the surface a faint moonlit sheet; everything else near-black navy, lit only where plankton flashes. Keep true blacks (black point under 20), saturated cyan-blue highlights, a gentle bloom on the brightest light only.

## Movement references

Turtle: 4.8 s stroke cycle, downstroke ~45% of it. Fish: tail beat ~5 Hz at ~2 body lengths/s; the school shape breathes and banks. Waves: ~7 s period, ~13 m wavelength near shore; the wash runs up and back over the slope.
