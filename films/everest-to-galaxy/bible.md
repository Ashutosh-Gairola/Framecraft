# Everest to the Galaxy — bible

A 3D film (`skills/html-anime-3d-actor/SKILL.md`): one continuous journey in six scenes, drawn by `assets/world.js` at film time through one shared renderer.

## Stages and seams

| Time | Stage | How it joins the next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–20s | Everest at sunrise: pyramidal peaks, snow, rock, glaciers with crevasses, a sea of clouds, blowing snow, the summit plume | The camera passes over the summit and climbs into cloud; a white veil covers the swap at 20.3s |
| 20–40s | The volcano: a cone over black lava fields, a crater lava lake with bubbles, six lava flows, fire light, a smoke plume, ash | The camera rises into the plume; smoke turns to cloud; the swap at 40.5s is under the veil |
| 40–80s | Earth and near space: day/night shader with city lights, ocean glint, a cloud layer, the atmosphere's rim, the Moon, satellites, stars | A 2.5s scale crossfade into the solar system through the starfield |
| 80–100s | The solar system: the Sun and its corona; Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter and its storm, Saturn and its rings, Uranus, Neptune; orbit lines at the end | A 2.5s crossfade as the Sun becomes a speck |
| 100–120s | The Milky Way: 210,000 stars in four logarithmic arms and a bulge, nebulae, dust lanes, a golden core; the camera rises and pulls back to a stop | — |

## Paints

The `indigo` script plus `ocean`, `land`, `cyan`; every color in the world is a paint or a blend of two.

## Motion

Cards: `effects.md` (snow, smoke, lava, light, cloud). Everything moves from film time: clouds drift, snow blows, the plume streams, lava scrolls and bubbles, smoke rises and spreads, ash falls, Earth and its clouds turn at different rates, satellites orbit, planets spin, the corona breathes, the galaxy turns.

## Sound

Wind on Everest; rumble, booms, crackle and thunder at the volcano; air and a drone into space; a synthesized score that builds from calm to climax and resolves serene.
