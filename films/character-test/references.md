# Character test — references

- **The reference man** (`references/man-reference.jpg`, git-ignored): "David Nikulin" by Innovation Lab, CC BY 2.0, https://www.flickr.com/photos/14895421@N00/6951874555. A full-length studio photo of a slim man in a black button-up shirt with the sleeves rolled to the forearm, dark-wash jeans, black sneakers with white soles, and short brown hair. The design takes the outfit, build and palette, not the man's identity. Colours sampled with `harness/refs.js palette` and region medians: skin #d6b39d (cheek, studio-lit), hair #382924, shirt #24201f, jeans #25262a.
- **Goku** (Dragon Ball Z, base form), from the character's well-known design:
  - spiked black hair: large spikes up and back, bangs over the forehead, a tuft standing up at the front;
  - an orange gi over a blue undershirt;
  - a blue sash, blue wristbands, blue boots with red ties;
  - black eyes under angled brows;
  - a heavily muscled build.

  The symbol on the gi is left out (no text).

- **Goku's outfit** (`references/goku-cosplay.jpg`, git-ignored): "Goku cosplay" by Hikaru Kazushime, CC BY-SA 2.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Goku_cosplay.jpg. What it shows:
  - two separate trouser legs that balloon to the knee and are gathered into the boots, with a puff over the boot tops;
  - a wide wrapped blue sash with long tails;
  - blue boots with red trim at the sole and a white tie at the top;
  - blue wristbands.

- **The realistic head** (`assets/scan/`): the Lee Perry-Smith head scan that ships with three.js. Credit: Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite Realities, based on a work at triplegangers.com, licensed CC BY 3.0. It comes with a colour map and a normal map. It is fitted to the skeleton with its closed eyes opened into almond lids, and given grown hair, lashes and corneas. It is a different person from the reference photo: the design matches type, build and palette, not identity.

- **Goku's face** (`references/goku-manga.png`, git-ignored, study only): Akira Toriyama's colour art of adult Goku, as used on Wikipedia's Goku article (non-free; not shipped). What it shows:
  - large white eyes with small black irises;
  - thick brows angled down;
  - an angular jaw and a defined nose;
  - spikes up, back and out at the sides, with bangs;
  - the blue undershirt's sleeves showing below the orange top, which is cut at the shoulder.
