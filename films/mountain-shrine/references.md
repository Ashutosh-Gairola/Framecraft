## Light and grade

A clear Himalayan morning: a deep blue sky, crisp snow, true shadows filled blue by the sky, saturated colour. The references sample at mid-tone saturation 0.23–0.39; the film is graded to ~0.3 with a black point near 20 (the first version sat at 0.26 and 40: hazy and pastel).
