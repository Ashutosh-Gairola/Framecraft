## Consistency (every shot)

- **Kael:** silver-white hair tied loosely at the back with strands framing the face; glowing ice-blue eyes; black high-collared coat; silver pauldrons; blue runes down the right arm; the cyan-edged katana. Energy is always blue-white.
- **Raizen:** black hair with crimson tips; glowing red eyes; a scar over the left brow; black segmented plate with crimson glowing cracks; three-layer pauldrons; gauntlets; a torn red cape; the greatsword with its red core. Energy is always crimson-black.
- **Heights:** Kael 1.84 m, Raizen 1.93 m, both 7.4 heads tall.
