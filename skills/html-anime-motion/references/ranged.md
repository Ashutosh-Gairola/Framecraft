# Ranged — bow, thrown weapons, aim

Stillness before release is the performance. The release is on ones.

## Bow, 6 keys

1. **Stance**: side-on to the target, feet a shoulder apart, bow arm hanging.
2. **Nock**: the arrow on the string at the nocking point, the shaft across the bow on the side away from the body (for a right-handed archer, on the left side of the bow).
3. **Raise**: the bow arm comes up to shoulder height, nearly straight, elbow turned out of the string's path.
4. **Draw**: three fingers on the string (one above the arrow, two below), pulled back to the anchor at the jaw or the corner of the mouth. The draw elbow rises level with the arrow. The bow limbs bend more the further the draw. Hold 12f+; a 2-frame tremble is allowed.
5. **Release**: the fingers open; the drawing hand moves straight back past the ear. The string snaps forward, the bow rocks in the hand. The arrow leaves on ones: one smear frame, then gone or a single streak.
6. **Follow-through**: bow arm stays up, eyes on the target. Hold.

The bow's pivot is the grip. The string runs straight from tip to tip at rest, and from tip to the drawing fingers to tip when drawn: two straight lines, never a curve.

## Thrown blades (kunai, shuriken, knives)

Use the throw from `handling.md`, faster and with a flick of the wrist at release. A shuriken spins: 2–3 drawings of rotation, repeated, on ones. A knife flips end over end on its own arc, or flies flat without spin.

## Aiming

The eye, the sight line, and the target are one straight line. Draw the line of sight before the figure. The body is still; only the eyes and a 1–2 frame breath move.

## Common mistakes

- The bowstring curved like a rubber band.
- The arrow on the wrong side of the bow.
- The bow arm locked straight with the elbow in the string's path.
- A long eased flight of the arrow. It is gone in 1–3 frames.
